🏦 Banks are differentUse P/B, ROE, ROA and NPA (bad loans). Ignore Debt/Equity, EV/EBITDA, FCF, Interest cover.
⚖️ Compare within the sectorIT/FMCG naturally have high P/E & margins; steel/banks low. A "high" number may be normal for that sector.
🔗 Read ratios togetherLow P/E + falling profit = value trap. High ROE + high debt = risky. One ratio never decides.
📅 Check the trendImproving ROE, margins and growth over 3-4 years beats a single great year.
Formulas use a sample company "Chai Ltd" (price ₹200, profit ₹40 Cr, equity ₹200 Cr, debt ₹100 Cr) in the explanations. Ranges are general Indian-market rules of thumb, not investment advice. Prints on one A4 landscape page.
